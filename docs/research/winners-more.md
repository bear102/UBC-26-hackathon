## 0. MBC (UBC predecessor): still mostly a dead end, plus one new UBC fact

- **The MBC 2024 and 2025 winners are still not retrievable.** Devpost (mbc.devpost.com, its project pages and participant portfolios) returns 403, x.com/mbc_conference returns 402, and none of 10+ searches turned up a recap, LinkedIn post, Luma page, GitHub README or student-org blog naming winners. The 2025 event was the *second* annual MBC (Dec 5-6, 2025, Michigan Ross; https://events.umich.edu/event/142064), so MBC 2024 was the first, and no hackathon results were published for it.
- **One confirmed MBC 2025 submission (whether it won is unknown) [?]: "Manifest."** It was a Farcaster/Base mini app prediction market on Base Sepolia. Anyone creates a yes/no market and bets USDC. **10% of every pool goes to the person who actually makes the event happen**, who claims it by uploading photo evidence (the example was pie-ing a named person). It also paid a bonus for guessing the exact time. https://devpost.com/software/manifest-2isylp (from a search snippet; the page itself returned 403). Takeaway: MBC 2025 entrants built *event-local, social, IRL* markets. The team should still open https://mbc.devpost.com/project-gallery in a real browser.
- MBC 2025 also ran a separate **MBC Research Competition** with six judges and onchain markets on who would win it (e.g., "MetaDAO from UT Austin", "Grass from Columbia"). https://explorer.struct.to/markets/will-metadao-from-the-university-of-texas-at-austin-win-the-mbc-research-competition

Finals: up to 13 teams pitch live on Nov 21. The page publishes no scoring criteria and no past winners. https://www.universityblockchain.org/programs/hackathon

## (b) Patterns (new, or confirmations with new evidence)

1. **Parametric "auto-pay when a measurable bad thing happens" keeps reaching ETHGlobal finals.** TX Delay Insurance (pays on tx delay, with RPC timestamps as proof), JetLagged (flight delays), Noah (dead man's switch). Judges like it because the trigger is objective and the payout is visible on stage. This directly supports idea 1 (an overcharge triggers the payout) and idea 3 (bump compensation).
2. **"Reward the signal, not the trade" is an emerging winner category.** Bench (Frontier top 25: pays for useful signals), Signals (Taipei: rewards accurate ranges), DIVE (agents verify the truth), and InfoFi (earlier: pay only above an accuracy threshold). Prediction markets are moving from order books to *information markets*. That is good for idea 2, but it is no longer a novel category.
3. **Agent-wallet guardrails are now a finalist-grade product by themselves.** ENShell (Cannes 2026 finalist: policy check before the wallet), Sudont (Frontier top 25: execution firewall). Combined with the earlier Mercantill and Latinum, "safety for agent money" is a recognized lane. Idea 4 fits it, but it needs a sharper hook than "a policy engine."
4. **Escrow and x402 extensions win when they fix a specific x402 weakness.** x402-flash (escrow for latency), zkx402 (privacy and authenticity), Kyma Pay (402 merchant flow with a fee comparison). The winners pick *one* flaw of plain x402 and fix it. x402 itself has no refund mechanism (https://stablecoininsider.org/refunds-when-an-agent-initiated-the-payment/), which is the gap idea 4 exploits.
5. **Offline / real-retail inputs differentiate.** Halo (paper receipts), ETHPark-QR (parking QR), Primer (Amazon), cellfi (SMS), LensMint (camera), Paybot and CrowdBrain (robots). Of ~60 finalists reviewed, roughly a third had a physical or real-merchant touchpoint. This confirms the existing "physical demo" pattern with a fresh 2025-26 sample.
6. **Named underserved user plus privacy.** Pace Club, PrivyCycle (women), cellfi (feature phones), Nomadia (no banks), KinnectFi (Philippine diaspora), LightWallet (blind users). Accessibility for blind users is rare among finalists. Only LightWallet was found, and it won a sponsor prize, not finals. That leaves room for idea 1, but no evidence yet that it wins main tracks.
7. **Colosseum Frontier (2026) judged startup viability explicitly.** Its stated criteria were "execution speed, insight, founder-market fit, prioritization" (blog). Its top 25 skew to prediction-market derivatives, TCG/collectibles, RWA and India/Philippines stablecoin corridors, with few pure "agent framework" winners. For the Solana chain award, frame the project as a company, not a demo.
8. **Event-local / IRL betting shows up at student and ETHGlobal events.** Manifest at MBC 2025 (10% bounty to whoever makes the event happen) and livestakes at Cannes 2025 (bets on hackathon teams) both build markets about the room itself. That suits a live-audience finals demo.

## (c) Prior art vs our ideas

**(1) Voice checkout for blind shoppers; overcharges pay the shopper from the cashier's escrowed USDC shift bonus on Arc**
- LightWallet (ETHGlobal, Mask Network prize [year ?]): a voice wallet for visually impaired users, with no checkout or overcharge mechanic. https://ethglobal.com/showcase/lightwallet-e2ici
- PayFlow AI (lablab "AI Agents on Arc with USDC"): voice "send 10 USDC to Bob" on Arc testnet with ElevenLabs. It is voice payments on Arc, but not accessibility-specific and has no penalty escrow. https://lablab.ai/ai-hackathons/ai-agents-arc-usdc/defai-trading/payflow-ai
- "Alternative POS Machine" (2020 hackathon): a voice assistant that double-checks the contactless amount for blind payers. It is the closest mechanic, with no crypto. https://atahancaldir.github.io/alternative-pos-machine-portfolio-details.html
- **Strong real-world hook:** **Michigan's Scanner Law** already pays an overcharged shopper the difference plus a bonus of 10x the overcharge ($1-$5), due within 2 days. Idea 1 is effectively "the Michigan Scanner Law, enforced automatically by escrow." https://www.michigan.gov/en/consumerprotection/Protect-Yourself/consumer-alerts/shopping/Michigans-Scanner-Law. Texas has no such law [?, not verified].
- No found project combines blind shoppers, escrowed cashier bonds and auto-payout. The idea is **novel**.

**(2) Agent pays only for prediction tips the market later moved toward (CLV), Solana + Gemini**
- **EdgeLedger** and **CLV Scout** (HackQuest / OKX.AI hackathons, 2026 [?]): x402 on X Layer that *grades* picks against the closing line ($0.01/grade, $0.20 tout audit, Sharp Score). They use CLV as the metric but charge for grading, not conditional payment for tips. https://hackquest.io/en/projects/EdgeLedger, https://hackquest.io/projects/CLV-Scout
- **Insider Streams** (Chainlink Convergence hackathon): sealed auctions for prediction-market signals, with seller reputation +1/-1 after resolution through Chainlink CRE. It uses outcome correctness, not CLV, and payment is not conditional. https://chain.link/hack-26/projects/insider-streams
- Optic AI (OKX hackathon): x402 returns 404 *before settlement* when it can't answer, so there is no charge for a non-answer. That is a partial "pay-only-if" precedent.
- Bench (Frontier top 25): rewards useful signals.
- **Verdict:** CLV-as-metric and pay-for-signals both exist. The **escrowed, conditional pay-on-CLV settlement** was not found, so the novelty is narrow. Pitch the conditional-settlement mechanism, not "CLV grading." The EdgeLedger and CLV Scout pages are the most likely things a judge has seen.

**(3) Airline-style bump list for AI compute during Texas grid emergencies, paid via x402 on Solana**
- Murmuration (hackathon, Claude agents): grid-operator and data-center agents negotiate GPU timing, and the compute side bids on power, using synthetic ERCOT/PJM data. **This is the closest prior art**, but it has no onchain payments and no per-customer bumping. https://rohandani.notion.site/Murmuration-Electric-Grid-Stabilization-3508f6faba7880b996d9cf68d2dc707c
- Flex Watt (SCSP hackathon): a priority ladder for data-center load, with no payments. https://jackthesparow.github.io/flex-watt-scsp-hackathon
- Real-world: Emerald AI (25% cut on 256 GPUs over 3 h, Phoenix), Luxor + Bentaus (first AI-GPU response to an ERCOT 4CP signal, <500 ms), and Texas **SB 6** (mandatory curtailment plus voluntary demand-response procurement). https://hashrateindex.com/blog/an-ai-gpu-just-responded-to-an-ercot-4cp-signal-for-the-first-time/
- JetLagged (ETHGlobal BA top 10) shows that the airline-disruption framing lands with judges.
- No found project pays *individual inference customers* to volunteer to be bumped. The idea is **novel**, and timely with SB 6.

**(4) Agent wallet: payments to new payees wait 20 s in escrow and bounce back if unrequested**
- Slowlyy (product, not a hackathon project): holds all outgoing transfers, including AI-triggered ones, in a pending window (30 min on testnet) before settlement. It is not new-payee-specific and has no "unrequested" check. https://slowlyy.pages.dev/
- WAIaaS "DELAY tier": time-locks high-risk agent transactions (e.g., a 900 s cancel window). https://dev.to/walletguy/delay-tier-security-time-lock-your-ai-agents-high-risk-transactions-50lc
- Agent escrow protocols with "24h hold for new agents" and auto-expiry refunds (nullpath / abbababa docs [?]). ENShell (Cannes 2026 finalist) does policy checks before the wallet. x402-flash uses escrow for speed.
- **Verdict:** time-delayed agent transfers exist. The specific **"payee must have requested it (402 challenge / invoice match) or it auto-returns"** rule was not found. The differentiator is the request-matching, not the delay.

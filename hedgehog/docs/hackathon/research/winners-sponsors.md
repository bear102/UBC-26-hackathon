## (b) What wins each sponsor's prize

### Circle / Arc
1. **Stated criteria**: HackMoney 2026 = "technical execution, product utility, and integration quality"; Cannes 2026 = "alignment with bounty requirements, quality of solution, depth of technical implementation, creativity." Use the prompt's exact bounty wording.
2. **Stack multiple Circle products.** Winners named specific products: arctan(x) used Wallets + Gateway + Bridge Kit + StableFX; Text-to-Chain used Dev-Controlled Wallets + CCTP; ArcFlow used Gateway. Circle's posts call out which products each winner used.
3. **Nanopayments are the hot product**: at Cannes about 40% of submissions used Circle Nanopayments, and 4 winners built their core value on them. Sub-cent payments that gas would otherwise make uneconomic (per-page, per-song, per-use VPN) won.
4. **Agents are the baseline, not the differentiator**: 97-98% of Arc submissions had AI agents as financial actors, and 56% were cross-chain, which Circle treats as table stakes. Winners stood out through a real-world hook: artists, offline/mesh, SMS-only phones, payroll. Circle praised "impact" (privacy, artist pay, emergency finance) and teams with mainnet plans or business validation ("construction clients don't care about blockchain").
5. **Hide the crypto**: Circle highlighted email onboarding via Circle Wallets, hidden gas (USDC gas on Arc), and familiar workflows (escrow, BNPL, payouts).

### Prediction markets (Gemini PM bounty proxy)
1. Arc's own track is "Prediction Markets Built on Arc **with Real-World Signal**". Both winners (Predict It!, PolyPOP) were consumer and social, with low-friction market creation. They weren't quant tools.
2. Aggregation and UX win big: Capitola (meta-aggregator) took $25k in Colosseum's Consumer track. Fora (group-chat prediction trading) took 3rd.
3. Agent-sold forecasts win sponsor bonuses: AlphaOracle sells per-query forecasts to other agents, and VibeQuant turns a plain-English hunch into an approved trade (Algorand/Alpha Arcade bonus).
4. No public Gemini-PM winners found. Gemini's PM API is public with no key needed for REST, so judges will likely expect real live-market data rather than mocks. This is inference.

### ElevenLabs
1. Judges (Mati Staniszewski, Rauch, a16z) rewarded **standout live demos** and **real-world impact**. GibberLink won unanimously on a jaw-drop demo. Procuro won despite a late pivot because its demo impressed.
2. Winning projects had **the agent actually talk to someone**: calling suppliers or businesses (Procuro, Dealwise), coaching a patient (Pep), a driver copilot (Roadmate). Voice is the primary interface, not a TTS add-on.
3. Accessibility and care themes win "Best use of ElevenLabs": Junction (Penn), Health Butler, and Timbre (overall). Voice serves native language and people who can't use screens.
4. LiHacks' ElevenLabs prize criteria: "most creative/unexpected use of the synthetic voice" and "how central ElevenLabs is to the project". Remove the voice and the product should break.
5. Competition is dense (34/41 Penn teams used ElevenLabs), so the use has to be distinctive, for example voice cloning of a familiar voice as in Halo for blind users.

### x402 / agentic payments (Coinbase, Solana, Algorand)
1. Real merchant or physical-world hooks win: x402 Shopify Commerce, PlaiPin (ESP32), Print402 (3D print), Juicebag Mail, Volt402 (solar power), Solana ATM.
2. Trust layers are a recurring winner theme: Erster (pay-per-evaluation trust), Liminal (route to a trusted provider), Chainlens (pay only after the response is verified), Amiko and Legasi (credit and reputation), AgentID. Algorand's recap stressed trust (reliability, delivery, audit records) and payments "without extra user steps."
3. Infra and dev tooling places well: MCPay ($25k), Corbits, MoneyMQ, AgentTab (batching micropayments).
4. Must be working end-to-end in the hackathon window (Algorand: "working applications built over 36 hours").

### QuickNode Streams
1. Stated rubric (2024): use Streams for **real-time AND historical data**, apply **filters**, and use the **Streams REST API**.
2. Winner Gas Guardian = Streams feed -> AI prediction -> user alerts. Best Overall paired Streams with Functions (robotics). Combining Streams + Functions/Marketplace add-ons scored across multiple prizes.
3. Evidence is thin (one hackathon, 2024). Treat it as directional.

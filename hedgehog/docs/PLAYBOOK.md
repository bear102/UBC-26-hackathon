# Hedgehog industry hedge playbook

Real small-business problems mapped to prediction-market contracts that Hedgehog can quote **today**. The data lives in `lib/ai/playbook/industries.ts`. `playbookIdeas()` (`lib/ai/playbook/index.ts`) matches a business description to industries using keywords (no LLM), resolves each idea's market query against the live market list, and feeds the results into the creative-AI step in `advise()` as seeds that are already "included". Each seed's candidates are real contract ids.

**Status (validated 2026-11-19 ~03:30 CST):** 20 industries (19 with a validated idea), **32 validated ideas**, and **27 rejected or watchlist ideas** (9 invalid, 9 not live now, 9 future source), each with a reason. Run `npx tsx scripts/validate-playbook.ts [City, ST]` to re-check the live gates.

## How an idea gets in (the recheck)
An idea is kept only if all seven checks pass:
1. **Exists live**: a contract in `listMarkets()` (Gemini + Kalshi) matches the query and has a quote.
2. **Direction**: the side we buy pays when the business is hurt. For example, YES on "$6 or above" copper protects an electrician, and NO on "BTC $62,500 or above" protects a crypto merchant. The tests assert this for every class of idea.
3. **Causal link**: the link fits in one sentence (`causalStory`).
4. **Basis risk**: written down (station location, timing, magnitude, pass-through lag).
5. **Price and depth**: the protective side is offered at 2¢-85¢, with at least 5 contracts at ask+2¢ in the live book.
6. **Timing**: the contract settles inside a window the owner cares about. That means 6 hours to 45 days, or the next two Fed meetings.
7. **Natural hedge**: we checked whether the business benefits from the event. Plumbers profit from freezes and delivery businesses profit from rain, so those hedges are rejected and the note goes to the owner instead.

## Season and month hedges: sizing and tiering (2026-11-19)

**Sizing a season hedge (team futures).**
- Loss = expected extra home nights in the good scenario × the revenue lift per night. For example, a Spurs bar loses about 5 packed home playoff nights if the run ends before the West finals: 5 × $3,200 lift = $16,000.
- `lossUsd` is the extra loss over the whole window compared with a normal season, not per night. Loss caps: 7 days of revenue for event risks, 30 days for month or season risks.
- The premium is bought once. It is charged against the monthly budget as premium ÷ months of cover (at most 6) and shown as `premiumPerMonth`.

**Sizing a month hedge (rain, diesel, power).**
- Loss = the extra days lost (or the extra cost) in a bad month. For example, a landscaper loses 3 crew days × $1,500 in a wet December; a 4-truck fleet with fixed-price contracts pays the diesel overrun until the contracts reprice.

**Tiering (the ladder the owner sees).**
- **Recommended:** the stage that matches the risk, priced near 35¢ and at most 60¢ (70¢ only if nothing else fits). Skip books under 50 contracts.
  - "Miss the playoffs": playoff qualifiers, or a wins rung.
  - "Run ends early": conference finals or conference title.
- **Cheap tail:** a worse-season stage of the same team (playoffs NO for a contender at 4-10¢). It pays only in the bad tail.
- **Broad:** a deeper stage (West champion NO at 60-70¢). It pays more often, but mostly prepays the payout.
- Never cover a season or month risk with a single game or a single day. Own-team YES is used only to fund a promo the business pays when the team wins.

## Natural hedges (tell the owner, don't sell the hedge)
| Industry | Peril | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Bars & restaurants (patio / dine-in) | rain | If a big share of sales is delivery, rainy days partly pay you back (delivery orders rise); size rain cover to the dine-in/patio share only. |
| Food trucks & trailers | cold | Hot food (birria, ramen, soup) sells better on cool days; only a freezing, windy snap hurts. |
| Coffee shops, bakeries & cafes | cold | Cool mornings sell hot drinks; don't buy cold-day cover. |
| Landscaping, lawn care & tree service | rain | Rain grows grass: a wet week loses billable days now but fills the schedule two weeks later. Hedge long wet spells, not single showers. |
| HVAC & plumbing | cold | Freezes and cold snaps mean burst pipes and no-heat calls: emergency demand you profit from. Don't hedge cold. |
| HVAC & plumbing | heat | Heat waves fill the A/C repair schedule. Don't hedge heat. |
| Delivery, couriers, rideshare & trucking | rain | Rainy days lift delivery orders (people stay in); rain is a natural hedge, don't buy rain cover. |
| Seasonal retail & apparel | cold | Cold snaps sell coats, boots and heaters; they help a winter-stocked store. |
| Auto dealers & repair shops | cold | Cold snaps kill batteries and tires: more repair tickets for a shop. Don't hedge cold. |
| Jewelers, metalsmiths & pawn | other | If you BUY scrap gold/silver from customers (pawn, we-buy-gold), a metal rally lifts your inventory value: hedge only net short exposure (fixed-price quotes not yet bought). |

# Hedgehog

> "Your drink's on us if the Spurs lose": hedged on Gemini, guaranteed onchain. Kalshi signs up bars one at a time over the phone. With Hedgehog, any Austin bar, coffee cart or food truck types one sentence and gets a promo it can sell in 30 seconds. The promo is priced from Gemini's live book and hedged with real Gemini contracts before the first sale. The customers' refunds sit in a Solana escrow and land as dollars the customer owns.

**UBC 2026 Hackathon** entry. Targets: Consumer Apps track + Best use of Solana (Consumer pool) + Gemini Prediction Markets bounty (primary prize target; 1st place = finals slot). Fallback track: DeFi & Onchain Apps.

Status: pre-build. The full build-ready spec is in [docs/SPEC.md](docs/SPEC.md).
